About

I am a postdoctoral researcher interested in synthetic aperture radar (SAR), inverse SAR (ISAR), deep learning-based radar imaging, compressed sensing, and integrated sensing and communications (ISAC).

Research Interests

  • Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)
  • Inverse Synthetic Aperture Radar (ISAR)
  • Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC)
  • Compressed Sensing (CS): optimization, sparse solution, and SAR/ISAR imaging
  • Deep learning for radar imaging
